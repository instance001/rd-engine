# RD Engine notices and copy guidance

`src/engine.rs`, `src/save.rs`, and `src/lib.rs` carry SPDX identifiers that match the crate metadata: `AGPL-3.0-or-later`. SPDX labels communicate licence identification; they do not identify a copyright holder. Existing notices must not be removed.

If you convey copied or modified RD Engine expression, review the AGPLv3 requirements and preserve applicable legal notices. Mark modifications in a prominent, accurate way. This repository adds no citation, branding, or academic acknowledgement requirement to the AGPL.

The project asks, as voluntary scholarly/professional courtesy, that genuinely derived methodology be acknowledged using the wording in `PROVENANCE.md`. Independent implementations of an abstract method do not become subject to the source-code licence simply because they resemble or were inspired by that method.

The RD Engine logo is not required for attribution. Do not imply endorsement by `instance001` or FMI.
